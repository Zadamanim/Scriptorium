Finishing touches to the candlelight.

- The spectacles cast a shadow again, pinned to the four places they rest on the desk: the bottom of each rim and the tips of the arms.
- The pounce pot's shadow now begins at the widest part of its base.
- The candle shades the wood around its own dish.
- The legs of the writing stand cast long shadows away from the flame, running off the edge of the desk.
- The ink bottle's shadow is broader as it reaches away to the right.
- With the book shut, chapter ribbons slip out from under the bottom of the cover instead of lying across it.
