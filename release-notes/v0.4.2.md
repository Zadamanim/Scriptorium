Truer shadows, genre shelves and moonlight.

- Fixed: choosing a font, size, ink, bold or italic now works on the book's title, the author line and chapter titles. Each of these lines takes the new look as a whole.
- Shadows on the desk are now the shapes of the objects themselves, cast away from the candle and stretching and shrinking with its flicker in step with the dark edges of the room. Lift the pounce pot and its shadow stays on the desk and slides away from it. The old blurry drop shadows are now small contact shadows where things touch the wood.
- The Library starts with shelves for My Books, Fantasy, Mystery, Romance, Horror, Science Fiction, Adventure and Poetry, and there are no more blank unnamed shelves. The bookcase ends where your shelves end.
- Pull a book from the shelf and choose "Move to another shelf…" to rehome it.
- The moon now hangs at the top left of the window and casts a beam of moonlight down into the room. The raven lands at the bottom left of the window.
- The columns in the window room and the Library run past the bottom of the screen, so you never see their bases there. In the reading nook and at the door they now stand forward of the wall, and the door rests on the floor.
- Reading in the nook: the tartan blanket along the bottom is gone and the room is less darkened.
- The page edges beside the open pages now line up with them, and text no longer hops a pixel when a page turn ends.
