Candlelight, page turns and tidier text flow.

- Page turns now carry the writing: the words stay on the leaf as it lifts and lands, instead of vanishing and reappearing.
- "Let the words flow" now clears old page breaks by default, so no page is left half-empty. Picture pages keep their place in the story. (Tick the box if you'd rather keep your breaks.)
- Click the line at the top of a page (the book's title or the chapter name) to open Pages & Chapters.
- The candle now casts soft shadows from everything on the desk, and they stretch and shrink as the flame flickers. The warm glow and the dark edges of the room flicker with it.
- The pounce pot no longer gets cut off when you shake it, and it sits on the desk instead of floating.
- New in the Spectacles: Background animations — Lively, Gentle or Still — for a calmer room. If Windows is set to reduce motion, the Scriptorium starts on Gentle.
