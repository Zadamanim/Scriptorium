Ribbons that turn, and pages that close up.

- Chapter ribbons and marks now turn over with the page they lie in, and slide to where they rest on the other side, instead of jumping there once the turn is done.
- New: Gather the words. When a page has two or more empty lines at the bottom and the writing carries on overleaf, a small "room to spare ⤴" tag appears in its corner. Click it (or Quill › Pages › Gather the words…) to draw the words back up so every page is filled as full as it will go, and spare pages are taken out. Handy after making the writing smaller. Picture pages keep their place, chapters still begin on their own page, and page breaks you made yourself are left alone unless you choose otherwise.
- The "overfloweth" and "room to spare" tags on right-hand pages sit clear of the page-turning corner.
