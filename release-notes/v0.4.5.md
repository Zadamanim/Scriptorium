The Bindery, and the last of the shadows set right.

- New: the Bindery. Click the inside of a cover, or right-click the outside cover or a chapter ribbon, to rebind your book:
  - Cover leather in oxblood, forest, midnight, royal purple, saddle, teal, plum, slate or raven, or mix your own colour. The book's spine in the Library takes the same colour.
  - Lettering in gilt, silver, copper, ivory, scarlet or ink, for the title, author and ornaments on the cover.
  - Ornaments above and below the title: fleurons, crosses, stars, fleur-de-lis, crown, crossed swords, moon, sun, shamrock and more, or none.
  - A colour for each chapter's ribbon.
  You can also find it by asking the crystal ball.
