Chapter ribbons and marks.

- Stone columns now stand in the corners of the room, so you can tell where each wall ends as you turn.
- The writing stand has rounded legs and casts a deeper shadow on the desk.
- On ultrawide screens, the glow along the top and bottom edges now fades out gently at the sides.
